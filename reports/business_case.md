# Corporate Treasury Business Case: Liquidity Stress Forecaster

## 1. Investment Thesis & Executive Context
Liquidity buffers represent a costly balance sheet insurance policy. Idle cash held in overnight central bank accounts or ultra-short government bills incurs negative carry relative to active treasury yield portfolios.

## 3. Financial Benefit Justification
1. **Carry Optimization ($2.40M in Base Case)**: Precision forecasting reduces the required overnight precautionary buffer by ~$160M, redeployed into 6-month commercial paper earning an incremental 150 bps spread ($160M $\times$ 1.50% = $2.40M).
2. **Avoided Emergency Borrowing ($850k in Base Case)**: Prevents draw fees, dealer spreads, and repo arrangement fees on emergency liquidity lines during unexpected month-end crunches.
3. **Treasury Capacity ($112.5k in Base Case)**: Eliminates 900 hours of manual spreadsheet liquidity ladder updating, freeing analysts for liability management and interest rate hedging.
