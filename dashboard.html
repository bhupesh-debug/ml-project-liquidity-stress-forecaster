Q-P3-001 Which forecasting model performs best against the holdout?
▾
Direct Answer: HistGBM Regressor (p50) performs best, delivering **0.073 WAPE** and MAE 6.62 on the 180-day holdout, outperforming the 28-day rolling mean (0.379) by 80.7% and seasonal naive (0.105) by 30.5%.

Q-P3-002 How reliable are the predicted upper-tail outflows?
▾
Direct Answer: Raw uncalibrated quantile regressions suffer from under-coverage (87.8%). Applying a conformal calibration offset ($\Delta = +7.03$) restores reliability to **94.4% empirical test coverage** with a compact average interval width of 19.8 units.
Evidence: Conformal quantile evaluation on 180 out-of-sample days.
Q-P3-003 Under what assumptions does the liquidity buffer breach?
▾
Direct Answer: The baseline buffer (2,886.2 units) breaches when outflows experience a +25% shock for 10 consecutive days or during month-end settlements (+31.9 units). Under severe stress (+40% tail shock), the buffer is exhausted by **Day 22 (100% breach rate)**.
Evidence: Reverse stress simulation requiring a 3,914.8 unit buffer to survive severe drawdowns.
